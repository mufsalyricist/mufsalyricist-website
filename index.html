ABOUT

Mufsa Lyricist is an independent artist creating every piece of music from the ground up. Writing. Composing. Singing. Producing. Mixing. Mastering. One vision. One creator. One sound.

Based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India. A one-man studio with a global dream.
